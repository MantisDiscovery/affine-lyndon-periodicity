Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, “Imaginary standard Lyndon periodicity in untwisted affine types,” for publication in Journal of Algebra and Its Applications.

The manuscript proves the remaining classical cases of the imaginary standard Lyndon insertion conjecture by arguments uniform in rank and alphabet order. Together with the exceptional-type verification and propagation theorem of Elkins and Tsymbaliuk, these results establish the conjecture in every untwisted affine type. The exceptional verification is cited as prior work, not presented as a new contribution.

We also complete the untwisted imaginary-flag connectivity statement and derive a type-independent formula for the exact increasing-chain preperiod. The first two imaginary degrees then recover the increasing positive system and its Dynkin parent tree. Further results concern primitive column lattices, zero-weight configurations, and effective construction of the words.

The supplementary programs contain exact finite checks. The all-rank classical and phase arguments do not depend on those supplementary checks; the exceptional parts use the separately cited prior computer-assisted results.

Authors: Pranava Kumar, Abhijay Gangarapu, and Manolis Kellis.
